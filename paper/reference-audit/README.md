## Source conflicts and version boundaries

- **ARC-AGI-3:** the PDF and arXiv record name **ARC Prize Foundation**. The captured Scholar entry instead contains `author={Foundation, ARC}`. It is preserved in the export archive but has not replaced the correct existing corporate author. This is an explicit exception to verbatim normalization, retained for review.
- **Publication years:** lmgame-Bench and Orak are indexed as ICLR 2026 papers. ABC is in the NeurIPS 2025 proceedings, whose publication metadata says 2026-04-23; Scholar exports 2026. Crafter is indexed by Scholar as its 2021 preprint even though the PDF identifies ICLR 2022. These differences are reported as versions/publication metadata rather than evidence of invented papers.
- **Title variants:** GVGAI-LLM's arXiv/Scholar record includes “Agents”; its current v3 PDF title omits that word. The bibliography preserves the actual Scholar record. Initials, name transliterations and `and others` also follow the captured record.
- **Non-paper source:** Gemini Plays Pokemon is an existing Twitch project, not a research paper. A related Scholar blog entry is a different work and does not justify inventing a paper-format citation for the stream.
